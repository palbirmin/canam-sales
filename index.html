            Connections

            Checked every minute while Chrome is open

              Sales are before tax and tips. In-store is Clover card and cash payments only — app orders that also print in Clover are left out so nothing is counted twice. DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub and Website & app come straight from each company's own site. Cancelled orders are not counted, unless the app still paid you for them. You get is what DoorDash, Uber Eats and Grubhub pay after their commission and fees, the promotions and ads you run there and refunds (from each app's own payout report). The website & app has no commission. Card fees aren't included.
